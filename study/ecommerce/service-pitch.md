# Service pitch — E-commerce brands

*What I offer a DTC / online store. Client-facing language you can lift into a call, a
proposal, or the website. Service-first: I sell the outcome, not the algorithm.*

## The one-liner
> I turn your store's order and customer data into decisions that grow repeat revenue and
> cut wasted spend — the analysis an in-house data team would do, delivered as a service.

## The problem I solve
As a store grows, the data piles up but the *insight* gets harder to extract. You have GA4,
Shopify, and an ads dashboard — all showing numbers, none telling you **why revenue moved,
who's about to stop buying, or what to do next.** That interpretation layer is what I
provide.

## What I deliver (services)
**Foundations — "know your customers & orders"**
- Customer segmentation (RFM): who your best, loyal, and at-risk customers are, with
  ready-to-use action lists.
- Retention & repeat-rate analysis: are customers coming back, and is it improving?
- Honest order economics: real (median) AOV, best/worst sellers, whale detection.

**Diagnostics — "why did it change?"**
- Key-driver analysis: decompose a drop/rise in conversion, revenue, or AOV into the exact
  segment driving it.
- Seasonality-adjusted trends: separate real growth from calendar noise.
- Product affinity / market basket: what to bundle and cross-sell, ranked by real lift.

**Forecasting & prediction — "what's next / who to target"** *(for stores with history)*
- Revenue & demand forecasts with confidence ranges for planning and inventory.
- Churn prediction: a scored list of customers about to lapse, with the reason for each.
- Customer lifetime value (LTV) modelling to set profitable acquisition budgets.

**Attribution & causal — "did it actually work?"** *(senior tier)*
- Causal analysis of a redesign, campaign, or price change — did it *cause* the lift, or was
  it seasonality? — with a built-in credibility check.

## How we work together

3. **Monthly retainer** — the recurring "what changed, why, who's at risk, what's the
