# Service pitch — SaaS companies

*What I offer a subscription-software business. Client-facing; service-first.*

## The one-liner
> I turn your subscription and product-usage data into a churn early-warning system and a
> clear view of what drives retention and expansion — the data-science layer without hiring
> a data team.

## The problem I solve
In SaaS, small changes in churn compound into huge swings in ARR — but most teams can *see*
MRR and churn without knowing **which accounts are about to leave, why, or who's ready to
upgrade.** Your product emits a rich usage signal that predicts all of this; I turn it into
action.

## What I deliver (services)
**Revenue clarity**
- MRR movement breakdown: new / expansion / contraction / churn waterfall.
- Cohort retention & Net Revenue Retention (NRR) analysis over subscription months.
- CAC payback and LTV:CAC by segment and channel.

**Churn & retention — the flagship**
- **Churn early-warning system:** a monthly scored list of at-risk accounts, each with the
  specific reason (declining usage, feature drop-off, billing failures) so your success team
  knows what to fix.
- Churn-driver analysis: what actually predicts cancellation, ranked and explained.
- Usage-trend monitoring: detect the moment an account starts slipping.

**Growth & expansion**
- Upsell targeting: which accounts are likely to upgrade, and why.
- Activation analysis: which onboarding paths and features lead to retention.
- Account segmentation: behavioural personas beyond plan tier.

**Forecasting & causal** *(senior tier)*
- MRR forecasts with confidence ranges.
- Causal analysis: does a given onboarding step or feature *cause* better retention?

## How we work together
1. **Free sample** — a churn-driver or cohort analysis on a slice of your data.
2. **One-off deep-dive** — e.g. a full churn analysis or MRR-movement breakdown.
3. **Monthly retainer** — the recurring churn early-warning + retention report your success
   and growth teams run on.
