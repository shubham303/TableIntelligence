# Service pitch — Nonprofits & the consultants who serve them

*What I offer a mid-to-large nonprofit or a nonprofit/fundraising consultant. Client-facing;
service-first; mission-framed.*

## The one-liner
> I turn your donor and financial data into more efficient fundraising and rigorous,
> funder-ready reporting — the data analysis a development team rarely has time or tools for.

## The problem I solve
Grant reports, impact reports, and board decks are recurring, high-stakes, and deadline-
driven — funding depends on them — yet most orgs have no data team. Meanwhile donor
retention (the sector's biggest leak) goes unmeasured. I make the reporting rigorous and the
fundraising more efficient.

## What I deliver (services)
**Financial & efficiency analysis** *(can start from public data — no data needed from you)*
- Multi-year Form 990 trend analysis: program-expense ratio, fundraising cost-per-dollar,
  revenue-mix shifts — benchmarked and forecast.
- Funder-ready financial narratives for boards and grant reports.

**Donor analytics — the retention flagship**
- Donor segmentation (RFM): major, loyal, new, and lapsed donors, with tailored ask lists.
- Donor retention & cohort analysis: who's staying, who's slipping, by acquisition channel.
- Lapse-risk prediction: a scored list of donors about to stop giving, with the reason —
  so re-engagement is targeted, not blanket.
- Donor lifetime value to prioritise cultivation.

**Campaign & channel**
- Which appeals and channels actually work (and, where the data allows, which *caused* the
  lift, not just correlated).
- Fundraising forecasts with confidence ranges for budgeting.

## How we work together
1. **Free sample** — a public 990 financial teardown, or a donor-retention snapshot on a
   data slice. Zero risk to you.
2. **One-off report** — a specific analysis (impact report support, donor segmentation, an
   efficiency review).
3. **Retainer** — recurring donor-retention + fundraising-performance reporting, and
   report-season support.

## Why me
- **The public-data head start** — I can produce a legitimate financial analysis before you
  share anything, so you see the quality first.
- **Mission-framed** — findings are efficiency opportunities ("every dollar saved on
  fundraising is a dollar to the program"), never judgments.
- **Computed and honest** — every number traces to real math; I scope claims to the data and
  never over-state.
- **Careful with sensitive data** — donor PII is stripped; I work on aggregates and behaviour.

## Best fit
Mid-to-large orgs ($1M+ budgets) and the grant writers / development consultants who serve
several orgs and bill for this work.
