# Delivering insights — the craft that actually sells

Your business is "a distribution problem, not a tech problem." The engine already computes
correct numbers; the money is made in **turning a number into a decision a client will pay
for**. This folder is about that craft — the skill that separates a $200 report from a
correct-but-ignored CSV.

## The core belief
> Clients don't buy analysis. They buy **decisions they feel confident making** and **time
> they don't have to spend.** The number is raw material; the finding is the product.

## The three failure modes to avoid
1. **The data dump** — 40 charts, no conclusion. Feels thorough, gets ignored. A client
   should be able to state your top finding in one sentence after reading.
2. **The vanity finding** — technically true, commercially irrelevant ("Tuesdays have 3%
   more sessions"). Always tie to money or a decision.
3. **The overclaim** — stating a public-data hypothesis as fact, or a correlation as
   causation. This is the fatal one: a single wrong claim ends the relationship and the
   referral chain. Credibility is the whole asset.
