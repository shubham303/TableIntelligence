# Service pitch — Marketing agencies (your primary vertical)

*What I offer a marketing/advertising agency. Two motions: a white-label analysis layer for
their clients, and overflow analytics for their own team. Client-facing; service-first.*

## The one-liner
> I'm the data-science layer behind your client reports — I turn each client's marketing and
> sales data into deeper, ready-to-send analysis, either white-labelled under your brand or
> as overflow your team doesn't have hours for.

## The problem I solve
You rebuild the same client report every month across your whole roster — a manual,
senior-time-expensive grind. And clients increasingly want more than "here's what happened":
they want **why it changed, what's next, and proof your work drove results.** That deeper
analysis is what I add, without you hiring an analyst.

## What I deliver (services)
**White-label client reporting**
- "What changed & why" decomposition: turn a metric move into the exact segment/campaign
  driving it — in your voice, ready to hand the client.
- Deeper SEO/PPC/email analysis: ranking-to-revenue gaps, spend efficiency, cross-channel
  attribution, seasonality-adjusted trends.
- Delivered on a clean, consistent template, on a monthly cadence.

**Advanced analytics your clients can't get elsewhere**
- Customer segmentation & retention (RFM, cohorts) for their e-commerce clients.
- Forecasting: revenue/traffic/spend projections with confidence ranges.
- Churn & lead-scoring models; product cross-sell (market basket).
- **Causal attribution:** did the campaign/redesign *cause* the lift, or was it seasonality?
  — the defensible answer that protects the client relationship at renewal.

**Overflow / on-demand**
- One-off deep-dives when a client asks a hard question your team can't staff this week.

## How we work together
1. **Free sample** — I take one client's data (or public data) and produce a finding, so you
   see the depth before committing.
2. **Per-report / project** — pay per analysis, useful for overflow and one-offs.
3. **White-label retainer** — I produce a set of client reports each month under your brand;
   one deal covers several of your end clients.

## Why me
- **You keep the client; I stay invisible** — white-labelled, in your voice.
- **Every number is computed, not guessed** — findings survive a client's scrutiny, because
  that's your reputation on the line, not just mine.
- **Fast and templated** — depth without a data hire or a blown deadline.
- **Honest about limits** — I scope methods to each client's data volume and never
  over-claim; one wrong number isn't worth your client relationship.
- **High leverage for you** — one white-label arrangement multiplies across your roster.
